# Dashboard Design Rationale

## Layout Philosophy
Every page follows a consistent three-tier structure: KPI row at top for immediate orientation, primary analytical visuals in the middle for exploration, and a closing section findings, action items, or detail tables at the bottom for takeaway value. This repetition trains the reader's eye after the first page, so by Page 2 they already know where to look for what.

## Navigation
A static right-side panel persists across all five pages, with the active page highlighted in dark charcoal. This keeps wayfinding constant and avoids the disorientation of inconsistent navigation patterns between pages.

## Page-Specific Structure
- **Page 1 (Overview):** 5 KPIs, 2 supporting charts, 3 finding callouts. Designed for an 8-second read, the thumbnail page.
- **Page 2 (Revenue Reality):** 4 KPIs, scatter + table pairing, 2 closing charts. Proves the core narrative: revenue rank ≠ profit rank.
- **Page 3 (SKU Intelligence):** 4 KPIs, quadrant chart + risk leaderboard, a dedicated cut-list breakdown with a gauge chart for category dispersion.
- **Page 4 (Operations):** 4 KPIs, bar chart + geographic map, then a mixed bottom row pairing a histogram with a text-and-scatter statistical proof cell.
- **Page 5 (Scenarios):** 4 KPIs, scenario comparison chart + protected core table, 3 strategic action cards.

## Color System
Three accent colors used consistently: teal (`#1D9E75`) for positive/safe signals, coral (`#D85A30`) for risk/negative findings, gray (`#888780`) for neutral/structural facts. No page introduces a new color outside this system. Background stays warm off-white (`#F5F4F0`) throughout, never pure white, never dark mode to keep the dashboard approachable rather than clinical.

## Design Intent
The goal throughout was founder-readability over analyst-density. Every visual answers one specific question rather than displaying maximum information. Charts are chosen for what they prove, not for visual variety.
